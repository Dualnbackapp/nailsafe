What you can do

NailSafe: nail biting tracker and habit support

01

Notice with Desk Mode

With your iPhone propped up and the front camera active, Desk Mode can signal when your hand stays near your mouth.

02

Keep a photo journal

Take regular photos with consistent framing. Compare your own pictures over time without a promised recovery deadline.

03

Find a different response

Open a short SOS routine and record the moments that trigger the habit. Return to your routine after a slip.

NailSafe is a habit-support tool, not a diagnosis or treatment. Seek professional help for injury, infection or significant distress.

Questions

What does it cost?+

The App Store and the app show the current price and available purchases in your local currency before confirmation. Trial availability depends on the offer and your eligibility. A free download does not mean every feature is free.

Is the app available in my language?+

These are translated website pages. Check the Languages section of the App Store listing for the app itself. Some content may use a different language.

How do I manage a subscription?+

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select the app to review or cancel an active subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
